These notes say what the English on this site means. They are not a word list
to paste in: render each term naturally in the target language.

- "distress" / "distress deal" / "distress listing" is an urgent sale below
  market price by an owner who needs a fast exit. It is never grief, emergency
  services, a disaster or a financial-hardship ruling.
- "secondary" is the resale market — a property sold on by its owner rather
  than by the developer. It is never a school stage, a subordinate rank or a
  stock-exchange listing.
- "off-plan" is a property bought before completion, directly from the
  developer. Keep one wording for it everywhere, including the spellings
  "Off Plan" and "off plan".
- "ready" next to a property means completed and available to move into.
- "handover" is the moment the developer hands the finished property to the
  buyer. It is never a handover of duties, tasks or a job.
- "post handover" describes a payment plan whose instalments continue after
  handover. "Post" here is never a job, a position or a published article.
- "bed" and "beds" in a listing mean bedrooms, not furniture. "bath" and
  "baths" mean bathrooms.
- "unit" is one apartment or villa inside a project, not a unit of measurement
  and not a teaching module.
- "property" is real estate. It is never an attribute, a characteristic or a
  possession in the legal sense.
- "studio" is a studio apartment, not a workshop, a recording room or study.
- "townhouse" is a house in a terrace, not a country or village house.
- "project" is a residential development by a developer.
- "developer" builds property. It is never a software developer.
- "broker" and "agency" are a real estate agent and a real estate agency.
- "boost" on a listing means paid promotion that raises it in search results.
- "DLD" is the Dubai Land Department, "OQOOD" its off-plan registration
  system, "RERA" the regulator, "AED" the UAE dirham, "sqft" square feet.
  Keep these as they are.
- "Parent X:" in an admin label means the item above X in a hierarchy.
- Brand and legal names stay in Latin script: East Property, East Property
  Advisory, East Property Brokerage LLC. Project, developer and community
  names stay in Latin script too — Emaar, Damac, Azizi, Jumeirah Village
  Circle, Business Bay, Dubai Hills.
